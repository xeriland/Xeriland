# Timothy J. Xeriland, PhD

**Interdisciplinary Psychologist | Human–AI Interaction | Quantitative & Computational Research**

My research explores human cognition, emotion regulation, and interactions with intelligent systems, with particular attention to the role of quantitative and computational methods in understanding human behavior.

---

## Formative Reappraisal

I developed **Formative Reappraisal**, an emotion-regulation approach designed to support re-engagement following disengagement in learning environments.

Originally investigated in my doctoral research, the concept continues to inform my work on human–AI interaction and adaptive intelligent systems.

---

## Research & Technical Projects

My projects explore the application of statistical, computational, and artificial intelligence methods to research and practical problems.

- **Statistical Simulation:** Monte Carlo methods, confidence intervals, and statistical inference
- **Predictive Modeling:** Applied quantitative analysis and machine learning
- **Human–AI Interaction:** Intelligent learning systems and adaptive interventions
- **Computational Research:** Python-based tools for behavioral and educational research
